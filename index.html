About Our Smart City

Where Ancient Heritage Meets Futuristic Innovation

Known historically as Bezawada and revered for the sacred Indrakeeladri shrine, Vijayawada is now redefining the urban experience with cutting-edge digital infrastructure and sustainable smart governance.

Digital Infrastructure

High-speed optical fiber backbone, city-wide IoT telemetry, and an Integrated City Command and Control Center (ICCC) provide live monitoring of water, air quality, lighting, and traffic flows.

Sustainable Urban Ecology

Solar-powered public utilities, rainwater harvesting reservoirs along the Krishna basin, zero-emission electric bus routes, and expansive green breathing zones foster environmental balance.

Our Vision for Tomorrow

To establish Vijayawada as the commercial, educational, and technological beacon of Andhra Pradesh—combining resilient economic opportunities with an exceptional standard of civic life.

Smart Transportation Ecosystem

One of India’s premier central junctions connecting Northern and Southern corridors, enhanced with automated escalators, smart luggage scans, and digital displays.

The planned Amaravati-Vijayawada light rapid transit metro system mapped to connect Pandit Nehru Bus Station (PNBS), Gannavaram Airport, and Penamaluru.

AI Surveillance

Important City Locations

Discover iconic landmarks, spiritual sanctuaries, historic monuments, and natural wonders defining the soul and skyline of Vijayawada.

Riverfront Landmark

Prakasam Barrage

Krishna River

A majestic 1,223-meter structure spanning the Krishna River, regulating crucial irrigation for the delta while offering a spectacular illuminated promenade and sunset views.

Open 24 Hours • Scenic Walkway
Spiritual Sanctuary

Kanaka Durga Temple

Indrakeeladri Hill

Perched high on the Indrakeeladri hill overlooking the river and the city, this venerated shrine dedicated to Goddess Durga is the spiritual heart of the region.

Darshan: 4:00 AM – 9:00 PM
Eco-Tourism Hub

Bhavani Island

Krishna River Delta

Spanning 133 picturesque acres in the midst of the Krishna River, Bhavani Island offers water sports, botanical biodiversity, treehouse retreats, and tranquil picnics.

Boating: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM
Transport Hub

Vijayawada Junction

Railway Central

One of the busiest railway gateways in the Indian Railways network, bridging the Howrah-Chennai and New Delhi-Chennai corridors with premier passenger facilities.

Continuous 24/7 Operations
Historical Monument

Gandhi Hill Memorial

Tarapet Area

The first Gandhi memorial stupa built in India, featuring historic stone-inscribed quotes, a planetarium, a toy train, and an exhilarating 360-degree vantage point of the city.

Visiting Hours: 4:30 PM – 8:30 PM
Archaeological Wonder

Undavalli Rock-Cut Caves

Guntur Road Border

Remarkable 7th-century monolithic rock-cut sandstone caves showcasing ancient Gupta architecture and a monumental reclining idol of Lord Vishnu sculpted from a single granite block.

ASI Hours: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM
